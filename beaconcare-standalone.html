Demonstration data – not a medical device. BeaconCare is an educational, rule-based decision-support prototype. All measurements are simulated; alert bands are demonstration settings, not NASA-approved thresholds. It never diagnoses and never replaces the crew medical officer or flight surgeon. In a real emergency follow your mission’s medical protocol.
